Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our Instagram Automation App ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our service through the Instagram API.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our App, we may collect the following information through the Instagram API:

  • Profile Information: Your Instagram username, profile picture, and basic account information
  • Direct Messages: Content of messages you send and receive for automation purposes
  • Story Interactions: Information about story viewers and interactions
  • User Interactions: Data about users you interact with, including usernames and message history
  • Voice Notes: Audio recordings sent through Instagram direct messages (processed locally)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To automate Instagram messaging and engagement activities
  • To track and manage conversations with Instagram users
  • To analyze user engagement and optimize automation strategies
  • To transcribe and process voice messages for automated responses
  • To maintain records of interactions for follow-up purposes

3. Data Storage and Security

We take data security seriously:

  • All data is stored locally on your device or server
  • We do not transmit your Instagram data to third-party servers except for necessary API integrations (ElevenLabs for voice synthesis, OpenAI for AI processing)
  • Message history and user databases are stored in encrypted JSON format
  • Access credentials are stored in environment variables and not shared

4. Third-Party Services

Our App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Instagram API: For accessing Instagram features and data
  • OpenAI API: For AI-powered message generation and conversation analysis
  • ElevenLabs API: For voice synthesis and audio processing
  • Telegram API: For sending notifications and alerts

Each service has its own privacy policy governing the use of your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Message history is stored indefinitely unless manually deleted
  • User interaction data is maintained for tracking and analytics purposes
  • Audio recordings are stored locally and can be deleted at any time
  • You can request deletion of all your data by contacting us

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we collect about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke access to your Instagram account at any time
  • Export your data in a machine-readable format

7. Compliance with Instagram Platform Policy

Our App complies with Instagram's Platform Policy and Terms of Use. We:

  • Only access data you explicitly authorize
  • Do not sell or share your Instagram data with third parties
  • Respect Instagram's rate limits and usage guidelines
  • Provide clear information about data usage

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Data Deletion

To delete your data, you can:

  • Revoke the App's access to your Instagram account through Instagram settings
  • Contact us directly to request complete data deletion
  • Manually delete local data files from your installation

Upon receiving a deletion request, we will delete all your data within 30 days.
